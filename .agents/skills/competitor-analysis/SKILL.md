# Competitor Analysis & Industry Benchmark

## Purpose
Analyze competitor products and industry standards to uncover established user expectations, identify friction points in current market alternatives, and define clear avenues for design differentiation without reinventing familiar ergonomic patterns.

## When to Use
- During Product Discovery & UX Research (Gate 1).
- When redesigning existing modules or assessing why users abandon specific workflows.
- When determining whether to follow an established industry pattern (e.g., standard timetable grid vs list view).

## Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1: Identify Direct & Indirect Competitors
1. Select 2–3 direct competitors (products targeting the exact same market and audience).
2. Select 1–2 indirect/analogous leaders (best-in-class products in adjacent domains known for exceptional UX in similar tasks, e.g., Linear for task flow, Stripe for checkout/invoicing, Notion for modular content).

### Step 2: Feature & UX Matrix Comparison
Build a structured evaluation matrix comparing:
- Information architecture & navigation density.
- Onboarding and first-time user experience.
- Primary workflow execution (number of steps, cognitive burden, validation feedback).
- Data visualization and reporting clarity.
- Mobile responsiveness and touch targets.

### Step 3: Identify Industry Conventions vs Opportunities
- **Conventions to Respect (Jakob's Law)**: Users spend most of their time on other websites; do not break standard mental models (e.g., shopping cart top right, notifications bell, search bar top center, filters on left or top-drawer).
- **Anti-Patterns to Avoid**: Identify where competitors fail (e.g., clunky modal cascades, confusing iconography without labels, dense unreadable tables, dark patterns).
- **Differentiation Angles**: Where our product will outperform (e.g., superior keyboard navigation, editorial typography, zero-delay optimistic updates, crystal-clear fee transparent breakdown).

## Deliverables
- `COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md`: Comprehensive teardown matrix, screenshots/breakdowns of key screens, UX strengths, competitor failures, and strategic recommendations for EduCore.

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Are comparisons based on actual user workflows rather than superficial marketing pages?
- [ ] Does the report identify why competitors made certain trade-offs?
- [ ] Are common conventions separated from bad design habits?
- [ ] Is there an actionable list of "What to Adopt" and "What to Avoid"?
