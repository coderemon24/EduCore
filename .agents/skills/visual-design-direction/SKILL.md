# Visual Design Direction & Creative Strategy

## Purpose
Establish an opinionated, high-taste visual identity for digital products. This skill prevents generic AI-generated aesthetics by establishing purposeful creative direction, artful composition, and brand personality aligned with the client's business context.

## When to Use
- In Phase 2: Visual Design Direction (Gate 2).
- When initiating a new brand kit, landing page, institutional portal, or dashboard theme.
- When existing UI looks generic, uninspired, or disjointed.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1: Brand Personality & Tone Spectrum
Define the brand across 5 foundational tonal dimensions (score 1–10):
1. **Conservative vs Progressive**: (e.g., Institutional Heritage 8/10 vs Startup Disruptor 2/10).
2. **Minimalist vs Rich/Ornamental**: (e.g., High-Density Utility 7/10).
3. **Formal vs Approachable**: (e.g., Authoritative yet Welcoming 6/10).
4. **Playful vs Serious**: (e.g., Rigorous Academic Excellence 8/10).
5. **Modern Tech vs Humanist Warmth**: (e.g., Humanist Warmth with Modern Precision 7/10).

### Step 2: Curation of Visual References & Moodboard
1. Source references from high-craft editorial design, print layout, and leading digital platforms (Land-book, Mobbin, Typewolf, Awwwards).
2. Curate 4–6 visual reference pillars:
   - **Pillar 1: Typographic Authority** (e.g., traditional editorial serif titles paired with geometric modern sans-serif body).
   - **Pillar 2: Spatial Rhythm & Grid** (e.g., asymmetric 60/40 layouts with generous breathing room).
   - **Pillar 3: Material & Texture** (e.g., crisp hairline borders, calm paper-white cards, subtle noise or linen undertones instead of plastic gloss).
   - **Pillar 4: Accent Economy** (e.g., single institutional focal accent with purposeful CTA highlight).

### Step 3: Layout Composition Principles
- **Ground the Hero**: The opening fold must immediately present the most characteristic element of the subject (e.g., live institution badge, flagship statistic, authoritative promise headline).
- **Break Symmetry**: Avoid uniform 3-card columns. Use asymmetric weights (65/35) to direct gaze naturally.
- **Visual Anchor**: Introduce an anchor element (e.g., floating trust credential or established date) that overlaps subtle boundaries to create depth.

## Anti-AI-Slop Visual Rules
- **No Cliché Color Washes**: Ban purple-cyan / magenta gradients on backgrounds and text.
- **No Plastic Glassmorphism**: Eliminate translucent cards with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and white glowing borders.
- **No Meaningless Sparkles**: Eliminate `✨` and decorative glow orbs.
- **No Arbitrary Bento Grids**: Every card in a grid must have distinct functional importance; do not chop content into arbitrary boxes simply to fill space.

## Deliverables
1. `DESIGN_DIRECTION.md`: Brand voice, visual philosophy, composition rules, and anti-patterns.
2. `DESIGN_REFERENCES.md`: Curated visual references with detailed critique of what to adapt and why.
3. `VISUAL_MOODBOARD.md`: Color mood, typographic atmosphere, imagery direction, and aesthetic anchors.

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Is the visual direction recognizably tailored to this specific client/industry?
- [ ] Could this design be mistaken for a generic SaaS landing page? If yes, refine and add domain signature.
- [ ] Are typography and color choices justified by the brand personality matrix?
- [ ] Is there an intentional hierarchy that guides the eye effortlessly?
