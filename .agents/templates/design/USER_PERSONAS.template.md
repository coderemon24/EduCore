## Persona 1: The Primary Operator
- **Name / Role**: Farhana Rahman (Head of Academic Affairs)
- **Demographics**: 42 years old, highly organized, uses office desktop (Chrome on Windows) and Android smartphone.
- **Goals**: Ensure accurate class scheduling, assign substitute teachers quickly, and publish error-free exam routines.
- **Frustrations**: Confusing routine clashes, duplicate entries, systems requiring 10 clicks to change a classroom.
- **Tech Fluency**: Moderate (expert in Excel, impatient with convoluted software).
- **Key Quote**: *"I don't need fancy animations; I need to know in 5 seconds if two teachers are assigned to the same room."*

---

## Persona 2: The End Beneficiary
- **Name / Role**: Kamal Hossain (Guardian / Parent)
- **Demographics**: 38 years old, busy small business owner, accesses system 95% via mobile on 4G network.
- **Goals**: Check child's attendance, pay school fees safely, download monthly test report cards.
- **Frustrations**: Unreadable fee bills with hidden charges, broken mobile views, slow-loading PDF pages.
- **Tech Fluency**: Everyday smartphone user (WhatsApp, bKash, Facebook).
- **Key Quote**: *"I just want to pay the tuition fee from my phone and get a clear receipt immediately."*

---

## Persona 3: The Classroom Practitioner
- **Name / Role**: Tariq Aziz (Senior Mathematics Teacher)
- **Demographics**: 31 years old, takes attendance on tablet or mobile between classes, records midterm marks.
- **Goals**: Mark 45 student attendances in under 60 seconds without lag.
- **Frustrations**: Tiny buttons where fingers accidentally mark absent instead of present; losing input if Wi-Fi blips.
- **Tech Fluency**: High.
- **Key Quote**: *"One wrong tap delays the whole class. Make the buttons big and confirm saves instantly."*
