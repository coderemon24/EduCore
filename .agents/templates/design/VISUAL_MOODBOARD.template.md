## 1. Aesthetic Atmosphere & Vibe
- **Keywords**: Prestigious, Calm, Trustworthy, Human-Centered, Disciplined, Scholarly.
- **Visual Texture**: Crisp paper-white cards with fine slate borders, natural daylight photography of students and faculty, no plastic 3D icons or generic AI illustrations.

---

## 2. Color Moodboard Swatches

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Visual Sensation / Psychological Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas** | Clean White | `#FFFFFF` | Fresh, open, breathing surface |
| **Neutral Surface** | Calm Slate | `#F8FAFC` | Soft contrast without blinding glare |
| **Deep Typography** | Oxford Slate | `#0F172A` | Authoritative, deep legibility |
| **Brand Accent** | Institutional Green | `#005030` | Heritage, growth, academic authority |
| **Conversion CTA** | Warm Amber | `#F59E0B` | Energetic, inviting, decisive action |
| **Notice Signal** | Sky Blue | `#0284C7` | Informational, objective, calm |

---

## 3. Typographic Mood
- **Headline Aura**: "We shape thoughtful scholars with modern discipline." (Structured, confident, unhurried).
- **Body Rhythm**: Generous line-height (`1.65`), dark slate on light backgrounds, natural optical kerning.
