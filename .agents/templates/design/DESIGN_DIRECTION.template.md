## 1. Brand Personality & Tone Matrix
- **Domain Identity**: [e.g., Prestigious Academic Institution with Humanist Warmth]
- **Tonal Anchor**: Authoritative, calm, structured, credible, approachable.
- **Visual Stance**: High-contrast editorial craft over noisy tech-startup clutter.

---

## 2. Typographic Concept
- **Display / Headline Face**: [e.g., Newsreader / Playfair Display / Outfit Bold]
  - Role: Grounded authority, classic academic gravitas.
- **Body & Interface Face**: [e.g., Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter / Noto Sans Bengali]
  - Role: High legibility at 14px–16px, open apertures, comfortable reading rhythm.
- **Typography Scale**: Strict 4-tier system (Display 40px+, Titles 20–24px, Body 15–16px with 1.6 leading, Meta/Kickers 11–12px).

---

## 3. Color Harmony & Meaning
- **60% Canvas**: Crisp paper-white (`#ffffff`) and calm neutral slate tint (`#f8fafc`).
- **30% Structure**: Deep institutional slate (`#0f172a`) for typography and framing.
- **10% Semantic Highlights**:
  - Brand Anchor: Deep Institutional Green (`#005030`) for headline accents & badges.
  - Action / CTA: Warm Amber (`#f59e0b`) strictly for admissions/conversions.
  - Notice / Status: Sky Blue (`#0284c7`) strictly for urgent announcements.

---

## 4. Anti-AI-Slop Visual Commitments
- 🚫 Zero purple/cyan gradients on text or card borders.
- 🚫 Zero plastic glassmorphism with heavy blur effects.
- 🚫 Zero decorative sparkle icons (`✨`).
- 🚫 Zero arbitrary bento grids lacking functional hierarchy.
- ✅ Crisp 1px hairline dividers and solid, grounded cards.
